## Context and goal

Global AI training platforms require rigorous, reproducible evaluation of model responses. I work on LLM evaluation and alignment, contributing directly to the quality of the trained models.

## Scope of work

- **Side-by-Side (SxS) evaluations** with completeness, prompt-adherence, and consistency criteria.
- **Structured fact-checking**, with evidence validation.
- **Error-pattern documentation** to feed back into tuning (RLHF).
- Consistent, reproducible application of response-quality rubrics.

## Applied skills

Critical evaluation of LLM outputs · structured thinking and rubrics · fact-checking · error-pattern documentation · RLHF contribution · work with international quality standards.

## Outcome

Direct contribution to reduced hallucinations and improved alignment of the evaluated models. This experience connects with my master's research, which evaluates the quality of requirements artifacts generated by different LLMs.

> **Note:** these are real, ongoing international AI-work experiences, not a first foray into the field.
