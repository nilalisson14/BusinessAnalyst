## Context and goal

Educational products needed reliable, always-available automated service. I built and evolved conversational chatbots for the education sector, from flow design to behavior tuning.

## Scope of work

- Definition of **use cases** and **business rules** for the service.
- **Conversational flows** in Typebot, with automations in n8n.
- Integration with the **ChatGPT API** for natural-language responses.
- **Iterative behavior tuning**, attentive to the educational context, to reduce out-of-scope responses.
- Hosting and operation on an own VPS.

## Applied skills

Conversational-product specification · flow design · automation (n8n) · LLM API integration · applied prompt engineering · iterative behavior tuning.

## Outcome

Fewer out-of-context responses and higher first-contact resolution in educational support, with flows combining deterministic rules and LLM generation.
