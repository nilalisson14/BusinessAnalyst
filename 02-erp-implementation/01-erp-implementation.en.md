## Context and goal

A packaging manufacturer operating with scattered controls that needed to migrate to a single ERP covering the operation end to end. I led the implementation from initial assessment to go-live.

## Scope of work

- **Requirements gathering** with the business areas.
- **Mapping of all processes** with BPMN, SIPOC, and Ishikawa: inventory, production, finance, accounts payable, and accounts receivable.
- **Data migration** from legacy controls into the ERP.
- **Go-live** of the system and **user training**.
- Tracking dashboards in Power BI and advanced Excel.

## Applied skills

Requirements gathering in an industrial environment · process mapping and redesign (BPMN/SIPOC/Ishikawa) · data migration · ERP implementation management · user training · indicator building (Power BI).

## Outcome

A unified operation in a single system, with mapped processes and migrated data, reducing rework and parallel controls. The manufacturer gained integrated visibility of inventory, production, and finance.
