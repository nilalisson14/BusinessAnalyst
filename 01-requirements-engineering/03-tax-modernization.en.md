## Context and goal

A state treasury department modernizing its tax systems. Beyond requirements engineering for the fiscal systems, I took part in designing a **taxpayer appointment-scheduling system** to organize the in-person service flow for tax services.

## Documented functional scope

- Requirements engineering for the tax systems (revenue and service).
- Scheduling-system design: service locations, service catalog, user profiles, and tracking dashboards.
- Taxpayer-service process mapping in BPMN (Bizagi).
- Business-rule and flow specification for fiscal services.

## Applied skills

Requirements engineering in the tax domain · process mapping (BPMN/Bizagi) · service-oriented scheduling-system design · profile and dashboard modeling · alignment between requirements and public-service operations.

## Outcome

Taxpayer service organized by scheduling, with structured services, locations, and profiles, and clear requirements for evolving the fiscal systems. The service-, location-, and profile-management patterns proved reusable — evidence of componentization in the requirements artifacts.
