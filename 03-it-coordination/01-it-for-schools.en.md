## Context and goal

A state education project that needed to equip and maintain the IT infrastructure of 365 public schools, plus support the company's 3 offices. I started as a Requirements Analyst and grew into IT Coordinator, taking on leadership of the field teams and service operation.

## Scope of work

- **Team coordination** installing software and hardware in schools.
- **Configuration of service and monitoring dashboards.**
- **Process mapping** for support and operations.
- **Help-desk (service desk) rollout** to structure support.
- Requirements work for the internal systems.

## Applied skills

Leadership and coordination of multidisciplinary teams · large-scale IT operations management · service-desk rollout · process mapping · monitoring-dashboard configuration · requirements engineering.

## Outcome

A technology estate across hundreds of schools deployed and monitored, with structured service-desk support and standardized processes. Growing from Analyst to Coordinator reflected taking on responsibility for people, processes, and operations.
